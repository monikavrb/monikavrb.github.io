# Welcome
Another tool to learn Chinese and Japanese with flash cards: HSK 1–5 Mandarin and Minna no Nihongo I and II.

The site root (`index.html`) is a landing page where you pick a language.
- The HSK flashcard app lives in [`hsk-flash-cards/`](hsk-flash-cards/): https://monikavrb.github.io/hsk-flash-cards/
- The Minna no Nihongo I flashcard app (lessons 1–25, with kana readings, romaji and example sentences) lives in [`minna-flash-cards/`](minna-flash-cards/): https://monikavrb.github.io/minna-flash-cards/
- The Minna no Nihongo II flashcard app (lessons 26–50, same features) lives in [`minna2-flash-cards/`](minna2-flash-cards/): https://monikavrb.github.io/minna2-flash-cards/

Each app saves its progress separately in your browser, so studying one book never changes your progress in another.

The Minna no Nihongo word lists follow the lessons of the textbook. The English meanings and example sentences were written for this app, not copied from the book.

If you click the speaker button and the voice stays silent here are some steps:

Windows: Settings → Time & language → Language & region → add Chinese (Simplified) or Japanese, and include its speech pack.
Android: Settings → System → Languages → Text-to-speech output → install Chinese or Japanese voice data in the Google engine.
iPhone/Mac: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Chinese or Japanese, and download one.

Work best using Chrome, Edge, and Safari :)
